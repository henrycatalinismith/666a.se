666a is offline

From October 31st 2023 to March 31st 2025 666a.se provided a work environment email alerting service. During that time, something in the region of 8000 email alerts were sent notifying union representatives, journalists, and central union officials in Sweden of filings at Arbetsmiljöverket.

There were also some very lovingly updated English translations of Swedish labour laws. Those are still available. Get on over to lagstiftning.codeberg.page if you're looking for them.

Operating this service taught me a lot about what Arbetsmiljöverket actually gets up to. Maybe you're curious about what kinds of patterns can be found in the data about Arbetsmiljöverket's diarium. If so, head over to arbetsmiljo.codeberg.page where I've published some data visualisation work about it.

Thanks everyone for all the encouragement and support throughtout the lifecyle of this project. I think together we showed that digitalisation both enables and demands a more modern interpretation of offentlighetsprincipen. The more the world accelerates and fills with data, the more inadequate mere availability becomes.
